## Derivative and distribution notes

The files embedded in BsCode are application-specific derivatives, not the
standalone Pixabay source files. They are:

- normalized to 1280 × 720 at 24 fps;
- transcoded to H.264 video in an MP4 container;
- stripped of all audio; and
- embedded as selectable Cinematic backgrounds alongside BsCode's interface.

BsCode does not present these files as a stock-footage download library or
redistribute the untouched source videos. The Pixabay Content License remains
the governing license. Creator credit is provided here for provenance and
appreciation; it does not imply that either creator endorses BsCode.

The machine-readable source URLs, original-file SHA-256 values, review
records, derivative settings, and expected output paths are recorded in
[`curated-videos.json`](curated-videos.json).
